# Stock Market Fundamentals & Financial Statement Summary

## Core Market Metrics
* **NSE & BSE:** Primary stock exchanges in India. The National Stock Exchange (NSE) hosts the **Nifty 50** index, while the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) hosts the **Sensex 30**.
* **Market Capitalization:** Total dollar value of a company's outstanding shares ($Market\ Cap = Total\ Shares \times Share\ Price$).
* **Valuation Ratios:**
  * **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio:** Share Price / Earnings Per Share (EPS). Indicates market expectations of company growth relative to net profit.
  * **Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio:** Share Price / Book Value Per Share. Compares market valuation to net asset value.
* **Corporate Actions:**
  * **Dividends:** Direct profit payouts distributed to shareholders.
  * **Stock Splits & Bonuses:** Splits increase liquidity by reducing nominal share price while maintaining proportional ownership.

## Financial Statements Breakdown
1. **Balance Sheet:** Summarizes Assets, Liabilities, and Shareholders' Equity ($Assets = Liabilities + Equity$).
2. **Profit & Loss Statement (P&L):** Displays total revenue generated minus operating costs and taxes over a specific accounting period.
3. **Cash Flow Statement:** Tracks net movement of cash under Operating Activities, Investing Activities, and Financing Activities.
